# RayPath SCPT brand guide

## Selected identity

The primary identity is **Cone & Ray** using the **Field Teal** report theme. The mark shows a copper seismic source at the surface, three refracted teal rays, horizontal subsurface layers, and receivers on a dark-teal borehole.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ray paths and highlights | Field teal | `#147D75` |
| Wordmark, borehole, and table headers | Deep field teal | `#163F3B` |
| Seismic source accent | Copper | `#C87941` |
| Secondary text | Mineral grey | `#5F746F` |
| Soft panels and logo background | Pale teal | `#E8F2EF` |
| Rules and table grid | Mist teal | `#C9D5D2` |

## Assets

- `raypath-scpt-cone-ray-horizontal.svg`: preferred PDF header and wide-format lockup.
- `raypath-scpt-cone-ray-icon.svg`: compact app icon, favicon, and small square mark.
- `raypath-scpt-cone-ray-monochrome.svg`: single-colour printing and reproduction.

## Usage

- Preserve the copper source dot in colour applications; it is the signature accent.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the source-dot diameter.
- Use the horizontal lockup at 32 mm wide or larger. Below that size, use the compact icon.
- Do not recolour individual rays or use the mark over visually busy backgrounds.
